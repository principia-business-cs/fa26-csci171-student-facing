# Setup Step 1: GitHub Account

You will use GitHub to receive and submit programming assignments.

## Do This

1. Go to https://github.com.
2. Sign in or create a free account.
3. Use a username your instructor can recognize.
4. Add your school email in `Settings` > `Emails`.
5. Send your GitHub username to your instructor.
6. Open https://github.com/principia-business-cs.

You may not see course repositories yet. That is normal.

## Done When

- You can sign in to GitHub.
- Your instructor has your GitHub username.
- You can open the `principia-business-cs` organization page.

## Need Help?

Send your instructor:

- your GitHub username
- a screenshot
- the step where you got stuck

Do not send your password.
