# Group Builds And Reflections

Most Wednesday group builds are practice for Friday.

## How Groups Work

- groups change during the semester
- sometimes the instructor assigns groups
- sometimes students choose groups
- one person may type while others help plan, test, explain, or debug

## What You Submit

Usually, group builds are not GitHub turn-ins.

If asked, submit a short Canvas reflection:

1. What did your group build?
2. What did you contribute?
3. What did you learn?
4. What question do you still have?

## Why This Matters

The goal is practice and confidence before the Friday individual assignment.
