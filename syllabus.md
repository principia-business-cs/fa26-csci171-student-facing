## Course Description

This course introduces programming and computational problem solving using Python. The course is designed for students who may not have programmed before.

Most programming work happens during class. You will practice in groups, then complete individual in-class projects that show your own understanding.

## Course Philosophy

Confusion is normal in programming. Errors are clues, not proof that you are bad at this. We will learn one new idea at a time, practice together, and build confidence through repeated coding.
